Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

This policy explains how NorwinLabs ("we", "us") handles information on norwinlabs.com and in NorwinInventory.

This website

norwinlabs.com is a static website. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not collect personal information. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.

Contact requests

The consultation form on our Consulting page does not send data to a server. When you submit it, it opens a message in your own email app addressed to us. We only receive what you choose to send. We use it only to reply to you and do not sell or share it.

Chat assistant

Our chat assistant is a scripted tool that runs in your browser. It does not use cookies or tracking. If you choose to send a request at the end, the details you typed (service, answers, name, email, phone and notes) are sent to us by email through Web3Forms, our form-delivery provider, and are used only to reply to you. You can close the chat at any time without sending anything.

NorwinInventory

If you use NorwinInventory, we process the account details and inventory data you and your teammates enter in order to provide the service. Payments are handled by Stripe; we do not see or store your full card number. You own your data and can export it at any time. See our Terms of Service for details.

Third-party links

Our pages link to other sites, such as GitHub, Steam and windhelm.dev. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Children

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post changes here with a new effective date.
