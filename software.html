NorwinInventory

Everything you need to know what you have

Built around the scanner you already own, with the safeguards a growing business needs.

Scan anything

Any USB or Bluetooth scanner works with no drivers or pairing. Or use your device camera. Receive, remove, cycle count or look up.

Trustworthy counts

Every change is a logged movement: who, when, how many. Several devices can scan at once and stock never goes negative.

Low-stock alerts

Set a reorder point per item and see what needs attention right on your overview.

Import and export

Bring your catalog in with CSV, and take your data out whenever you like. Search and filter by SKU, category or location.

Built for teams

Invite teammates by email. Admins manage billing and team; members scan and edit stock. One shared inventory.

Simple billing

One flat subscription handled through Stripe, with a customer portal to update or cancel anytime.

24/7 customer support

Stuck at 2 a.m. during a cycle count? Help is available around the clock, every day of the year, and it is included in your subscription.

How it works

Up and scanning in three steps

  1. 1

    Add your items

    Import a CSV or create items one by one, with SKU, barcode, location and reorder point.

  2. 2

    Invite your team

    Send email invites and assign admin or member roles.

  3. 3

    Start scanning

    Plug in a scanner or open the camera, and stock updates as you go.
