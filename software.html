NorwinInventory

Always know what you have in stock

Built around the barcode scanner you already own, and it works on any phone, tablet or computer. You can even install it like an app.

Scan anything

Plug in almost any barcode scanner and it just works, with nothing to install. No scanner? Your phone or tablet camera reads barcodes too. Add stock, remove stock, count what is on the shelf, or look up an item.

A full history

Every change is written down: who made it, when, and what the count was afterwards. Scans from several devices at the same time never get lost.

Never run out by surprise

Choose how low is too low for each item. What is running low or sold out shows up the moment you open the app.

Ring up sales

Scan items at the register and the stock comes off by itself. Sales tax is added for you, and you can email a receipt.

Stocktakes and more than one location

Count a shelf, a room or the whole shop by scanning, review every difference, then update your stock in one click. Keep stock in several places and move it between them.

Reorder without the spreadsheet

See what is running low, turn it into orders for each supplier, email them, and receive the delivery straight into your stock.

Expiry dates

For food and other short-life items, track each batch and its expiry date. See what is about to expire, soonest first, and write off what has gone bad.

Print your own barcode labels

Pick your items, choose a label size and print labels for shelves, bins and products. Each one scans with any scanner or phone camera.

Contracts and agreements

Keep track of warranties, leases, subscriptions and service agreements: what they cost, what they say and when they end, with email reminders.

Keeps working with no signal

Bad reception in the stockroom? Scans are saved on your device and sent the moment you are back online, and never counted twice.

Your whole team, one price

Invite as many people as you like. Members scan and update stock; Store Owners also handle billing and deletions. You can even add your own name, logo and colours.

Bring your spreadsheet

Import your existing list in a minute, and take your data with you any time. Search by name or item number, or filter by category or location.

24/7 customer support

Stuck at 2 a.m. while counting stock? Help is available around the clock, every day of the year, at no extra cost.

How it works

Up and scanning in three steps

  1. 1

    Add your items

    Upload your list from a spreadsheet, or add items one at a time. You can even scan a product's barcode straight into the form.

  2. 2

    Invite your team

    Send email invites. Add as many people as you like.

  3. 3

    Start scanning

    Receive deliveries, ship orders and count shelves with a scanner or your camera. Everyone sees the same numbers, instantly.
