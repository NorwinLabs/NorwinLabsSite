NorwinInventory

Always know what you have in stock

Built to work with the barcode scanner you already own, with the safeguards a growing business needs.

Scan anything

Plug in almost any barcode scanner and it just works, with nothing to install. No scanner? Use your phone or tablet camera. Add stock, remove stock, count what is on the shelf, or look up an item.

Trustworthy counts

Every change is written down: who made it, when, and how many. Several people can scan at the same time, and the count can never drop below zero.

Low-stock alerts

Choose how low is too low for each item, and see what needs reordering as soon as you log in.

Easy to move in and out

Bring in your existing list from a spreadsheet, and take your data with you any time. Search by name or item number, or filter by category or location.

Built for teams

Invite your team by email. Admins handle billing and who has access; everyone else can scan and update stock. Everyone sees the same up-to-date list.

Simple billing

One flat monthly price. Payments are handled securely by Stripe, and you can update your card or cancel any time from your account.

24/7 customer support

Stuck at 2 a.m. while counting stock? Help is available around the clock, every day of the year, at no extra cost.

How it works

Up and scanning in three steps

  1. 1

    Add your items

    Upload your list from a spreadsheet, or add items one at a time with a name, barcode, shelf location and a low-stock warning level.

  2. 2

    Invite your team

    Send email invites and choose what each person can do.

  3. 3

    Start scanning

    Plug in a scanner or open your camera, and your counts update with every scan.
