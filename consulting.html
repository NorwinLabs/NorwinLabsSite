Services

What I can help with

Five areas of expertise, for small businesses, home offices and individuals.

Computer networking

Reliable, secure networks sized for how your business actually works, whether that is a single office or a few locations.

  • Network design and planning
  • Router, switch and Wi-Fi setup
  • Coverage and performance tuning
  • Firewalls, segmentation and guest networks
  • VPN and remote access
  • Cabling guidance and documentation

Website building

Fast, modern, mobile-friendly websites that look professional and are simple to keep up. This very site is an example: static, accessible and built to load quickly.

  • Business and portfolio sites
  • Responsive design and accessibility
  • Performance and basic SEO
  • Domain, DNS and hosting setup
  • GitHub Pages and static hosting
  • Ongoing updates and maintenance

Custom PC building

A computer built around what you actually do, whether that is gaming, creative work, development or a dependable office workstation, within your budget.

  • Requirements and budget planning
  • Parts selection and compatibility checks
  • Assembly, cable management and cooling
  • BIOS setup, OS install and drivers
  • Stress testing before handoff
  • Upgrades to existing machines

PC troubleshooting

Slow, crashing or not booting? I find the root cause instead of guessing, fix it, and explain what happened so it stays fixed.

  • Slow performance and freezes
  • Boot failures and blue screens
  • Hardware diagnosis and replacement
  • Malware and unwanted software cleanup
  • Data backup and recovery guidance
  • Printer, peripheral and software issues

Tech recommendations for business acquisition

Buying a business means inheriting its technology. I review the systems and give you a clear, honest picture of what you are getting and what it will cost to fix or modernize.

  • IT and equipment inventory review
  • Network and security assessment
  • Software, licensing and subscription audit
  • Risks, end-of-life systems and hidden costs
  • Upgrade roadmap and budget estimate
  • Post-purchase transition and setup
