Introducing NorwinInventory

Always know what you have in stock, without the spreadsheet.

A simple app that keeps track of what your business has on the shelf. Scan a barcode to add or remove stock, ring up sales, keep customer accounts with store credit, count your shelves and reorder from suppliers. It only shows the tools your kind of business needs. It works with the scanner you already have, or just your phone camera.

Meet Gavin

Quality is the standard, not an extra.

I'm Gavin Norwood, the person behind NorwinLabs. By day I design and run business computer networks and look after hundreds of company computers, and I bring that same discipline to everything we build and every client we help.

I hold my work to a very high bar for IT standards. If it is not secure, documented and tested, it is not finished, and I would rather take the time to do it right than hand over something that merely works.

  • Secure by defaultStrong passwords and logins, regular updates and virus protection are included from the start, not sold as extras.
  • Documented and labeledNeat, labeled cables, simple diagrams and clear notes, so anyone can pick up where I left off.
  • Tested before handoffEverything is checked and pushed to its limits before I hand it over. A job is not done until I have confirmed it works.
  • Honest communicationPlain-English explanations and straight answers, even when something needs to be redone.
